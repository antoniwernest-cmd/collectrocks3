# Adventure Reading — E, F, G, L, M, N, P, R

Open index.html to choose Soap Box Racer, Obstacle Course, Canoe Trip, or Rock Collecting. Each has four original level versions and follows the teacher-linked Mountain Biking assessment. Keep all folders together when copying or publishing. No installation or server is required.

The E/F/L/P labels are original instructional targets for teacher review; they are not Fountas & Pinnell certified benchmarks. Leveling uses a combination of characteristics, not word count alone. See [Fountas & Pinnell assessment FAQ](https://www.fountasandpinnell.com/faqs/assessment).

P has ten substantial reading pages per story; the other targets have four chapters. All versions include draggable cloze, questions, matching, a themed picture notebook, saved page locks, content-sized answer pads, and a spinning lock when ready. Voice is off by default. A teacher must enter override 259 and choose Enable voice. Voice directions then reads instructions only. Voice is turned off again when reopened or reset. Student text and choices remain silent. Teacher code 259 opens the original detailed report and exports.

Source: work/adventure-reading-eflp/p-ten-pages.cjs, reader-pages.js, reading-layout.js, stories.cjs, rocks.cjs, parent-report.js and build.cjs. Original template: outputs/Reading Assessments/mountain-biking.

Updated question structure: compact single-answer tick choices, missing-word dropdowns, story-idea dropdowns and draggable words with landing pads below Pixar-style pictures. Every answer section has its own Save & lock; all answer sections must be locked before the next page opens. E/F/G also include picture descriptions, with six rock descriptions at the end of Rock Collecting. L/M/N/P/R add more cloze, grammar, contractions and tense challenges. E uses shorter CVC-rich passages; G has distinct connected passages. E, F and G notebook questions include generated Pixar-style 3D scene illustrations.

Current activities: every assessment section is numbered and has its own Save & lock. E/F/G have four picture descriptions per chapter; the final Rock Collecting chapter adds six three-attribute rock descriptions. L/M/N/P/R add six cloze items and three each for grammar, contractions and tenses per chapter. Full story text remains visible. Responses are committed before changing tools and concurrent browser copies merge field edits. Andika is bundled for offline use under the included SIL Open Font License (assets/fonts/OFL.txt).

E, F and G each finish with a story-related diagram containing six draggable labels and six numbered landing pads. Labels support mouse, touch and keyboard placement, can be moved, remain saved after reopening, and use Lock and save when all six pads are filled. Earlier completed three- or four-label records keep their original diagram marks.

Student navigation: What’s left? is available on each assessment screen. It lists unanswered items and sections that still need Save & lock, and opens the relevant section or missing field. Next page stays visible: it opens this checklist until every required answer section is saved, then continues normally. The final diagram also shows its empty-pad numbers. A saved section with a missing response can be reopened for repair without clearing its other answers. The story-picture acknowledgement is optional and does not block advancement.
