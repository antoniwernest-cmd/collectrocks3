# Rock story background

The story introduces observing colour, texture, grains, measurements and mineral specimens, while distinguishing descriptions from unconfirmed identifications. The children examine loose garden stones supplied for their collection.

Geology facts were checked against [USGS Collecting Rocks](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/collect1/collectgip.html) and [USGS What Type of Rock Is It?](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/acidrain/type.html). The characters, dialogue, observations and garden setting are original fiction.
